### 7.3 data regeneration (user focus)

**D1. The sync refreshes about 100 to 112 entries per hour with the cookie, not 200. A full rotation of the regenerated list takes about 31 to 34 h, which misses UJ-6's "within a day".**
- **Evidence:**
  - The 7.3 spec computes the rotation from the nominal search limit: "3,452 searches is about 34.5 h without the cookie and about 17.3 h with it" (`spec-7-3-…md:82`, `:100`). The Story 7.3 AC asks for "the time of a full refresh rotation at the sync's request budget" (`docs/epics.md:3263`).
  - Live throughput: `sync-progress.json` `completed` grows by about 402 per 4 h tick across 8 consecutive ticks on `origin/master` (252 → 654 → 1057 → 1459 → 1860; 77 → 479 → 882). Each tick's `sync-report.json` shows about 800 `tracked-list` requests (one search and one fetch per entry, `packages/sync/src/pricing/price-entry.ts`).
  - The cookie is live. The Pi journal prints `pnpm sync: authenticated` at every session start from 2026-11-14 12:01 to 2026-11-15 12:02, and no `authHoldOffUntil` is published.
  - The Pi journal for 2026-11-15 12:02–12:18 shows one entry completed about every 32 s, each followed by `waiting until … (spreading requests over the rate-limit buckets)` of about 27 s.
  - **Likely cause (inferred, not proven by a test):** AD-8 decides the even spread on purpose: "each bucket's remaining capacity spread evenly over that bucket's period, so no bucket fills in normal use" (spine, AD-8, about line 471; `spreadBeforeNext` at `packages/sync/src/trade/ledger.ts:139`). The spacing is `limit.seconds / (limit.hits − used.hits)`. Under a rolling window it settles where `used ≈ hits / 2`, so the session runs near half of each bucket's capacity. Half of the 200/h cookie search budget is 100/h, which is what the Pi delivers. The code does what AD-8 says.
  - **The conflict is between a PRD promise and an AD.** SM-C1 (`prd.md:712`) holds the list at about 3,500 searches and promises a full refresh "within a day" with the cookie and in "about a day and a half" without it. UJ-6 (`prd.md:71`) repeats both. Those figures are the nominal budget (3,500 / 200 ≈ 17.5 h, 3,500 / 100 = 35 h), and AD-8's spread delivers about half of it.
  - The pre-epic list of 2,186 entries wrapped in about 20 h (`a604738` → `ca797d9`), within a day only because it sat well under the cap. Story 7.3 grew the list to 3,452, close to the SM-C1 cap, and that exposed the gap: 3,452 / 112 ≈ 31 h, and 3,452 / 100.5 ≈ 34 h.
  - Without the cookie, the same spread would give about 50 searches/h, about 69 h. That figure is inferred and not measured, so the no-cookie clause probably fails too.
- **Decision (user, 2026-11-15):** accept about 36 h. Action item 1 edits UJ-6 and SM-C1. The options were weighed as follows. The Story 7.3 AC (`docs/epics.md:3264`) gives the curator this decision when the rotation misses UJ-6. The options are:
  - (a) An AD-8 amendment for the architect, so the spread uses the full budget. For example, spread the remaining capacity over the window's remaining time, not its whole period. Then a sync story implements it.
  - (b) Prune rows down to what about 100 entries/h refills in a day.
  - (c) Accept about 31 h. That changes a promise the player relies on, so it needs a UJ-6 and SM-C1 edit in `prd.md` and a `PRODUCT.md` refresh.
  - The league starts on 2026-12-11.
- **Next time:** a rotation estimate is measured from the live `sync-progress.json` throughput, not derived from a nominal rate limit.

**R1 (ADV-1, medium). `tracked:check` can pass an entry that `rank` never counts (the 7.1/7.2 boundary).**
- **Evidence:** `classUnreachable` (`packages/sync/src/curation/check.ts:217-220`) counts an entry as reached if any recipe reaches it at any offered level. `recipeReach` (`packages/core/src/probability.ts:236`) does not test the entry's floor. `rank` counts an entry only at levels at or above its floor (`packages/core/src/rank-crafted-row.ts:70`).
- **Failure scenario:** an entry with floor 82 contains a lower tier. At 79 that tier is the best tier of its modifier, so the 7.1 per-modifier recipe floor keeps it and the entry is reached. At 82 a higher tier exists, so the recipe floor drops the lower one and the entry is not reached. The check passes, but the entry counts at no level. It still costs one search and one fetch per rotation.
- **This instance:** fix now. Skip levels below `entry.itemLevelMin` in `isReached`, and add a test for the floor-82, reached-only-at-79 case.
- **Next time:** none beyond the fix. The boundary review found it.
