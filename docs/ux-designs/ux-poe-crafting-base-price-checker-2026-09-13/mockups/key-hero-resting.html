Left: greater active. The word is the control — no select, no segmented control, no pill, no chevron, the same argument that made the threshold figure its own input. The active word is ink at 700 on a solid sepia rule; the inactive word is tertiary and is the only click target, because there is nothing to switch to on the active one. Switching reorders the list and rewrites every crafted row's Chase Combinations, since a recipe changes which outcomes are reachable and not only what an attempt costs (FR-26). It is not debounced: a click is one deliberate act.

Right: the recipe is uncostable. A recipe naming a currency with no current rate for the active league is uncostable, never costed at zero (FR-26, AD-20), so the cost line holds the money-slot phrase no figure yet. A zero here would be the worst lie the money slot exists to prevent — it would not look missing, it would look free, and it would inflate every EV on the page by the cost of the craft. What the crafted rows do in that state is deliberately unspecified and must not be invented from this render: an EV needs a Craft Cost, and whether those rows carry a money phrase, leave the ordering, or move to the appendix is a question about the ranking, which is core's and which the PRD does not answer.

The unit glyphs. ≡ an Item Class, ▪ a Base Type. They are in sepia — structural, meaning nothing — and may never take ochre or rust: a unit says nothing about whether a figure is sound, and these appear on twenty rows in twenty, so an ink here would end silence-means-healthy. They are the page's only wordless glyphs and take no key-block entry, because a unit can be read off the name beside it while a state cannot — the glyph makes it instant rather than making it possible.
