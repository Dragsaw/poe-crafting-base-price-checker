Impeccable critique — mockups/key-redesign-dark.html (v9) (9 unmerged: 2 high · 4 medium · 3 low)

reviews/review-impeccable.md

Dual-agent run (design review + detector and browser evidence) at 1000 and 1168px, judged against DESIGN.md rev 17. Design Health Score 30/40 (Good). Verdict in the reviewer's words: "a calm, disciplined page that sits well beside a dark game. In the left 350px, a player can read rank, name and EV from across the desk. The weak half is the right side." The colour and vocabulary are product-specific; the structure is a generic dark data table, "so drift in either costs more here than usual".

What holds, by measurement: header 64px sticky, row/line/header heights, grid columns, panel padding, radii and shadows all match tokens; every text colour matches its token; no horizontal overflow; the contrast floor holds (lowest 4.61:1) and the non-colour cue (NFR-10) holds for every distinction. The detector's 38 findings were judged false positives except partial em-dash overuse. Dropped under the Accessibility Floor: D1 (no keyboard path).

Merged into rubric entries: F5 → Token completeness; F6 → Visual reference coverage (memlog 269); F9 → Visual reference coverage (copy drift).

Persona red flags (mid-session glancer): ◐ at 13px from a fallback face blurs toward a dot at an angle; bold white tiers are the loudest text right of the EV column; the problem alarm is a 12px label in the far corner, and while a problem holds the age disappears exactly when freshness matters.

Open questions for the UX owner: (1) chase column at target width — two readable cells, three cut, or three with quieted tiers; (2) should one unreliable line carrying most of a top-five row's EV mark the row; (3) where the header's ~20px comes from; (4) is a 12px count in the far corner loud enough (memlog 254, 270).

Minor observations (not counted): trade-link hover, .more pointer and inactive-segment hover are undrawn; Emerald is singular among plural Item Class labels; negative EV beside a chase combination reads as a contradiction; specimen annotation colours sit near magic blue; expansion combination cells lack nowrap; Body Armours (Str/Dex/Int) and the longest trust reason fit with 0px to spare; the footer wraps to three lines at 1000px; the rounded scale lacks the slider's 2px.
