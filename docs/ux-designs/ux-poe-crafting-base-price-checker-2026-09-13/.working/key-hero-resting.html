The same strip in the frame above, with a bad health figure in the data. The five plain facts do not change — they are attribution, not health signals, and an old Tracked List edit date never turns red. A third line is raised, carrying a rust mark with its glyph, its word and its count. There are exactly two triggers and they share the one line: ✕ 12 unresolvable (FR-24) and ✕ pinned entries starved this run (FR-17, FR-25). A healthy sync raises no line at all — no count of nothing, no green tick, no “0 unresolvable”. Data raises this line, never a click, so it costs frame-reserve-health-line (21px) against the resting budget rather than against the expansion budget.

Contracts verified on this render. Frame: 1060 − (2 × 24) = 1012 = content-width, with the edge as a 1px outline that consumes no width. Ranked row: 32 + 222 + 84 + 88 + 94 + 492 = 1012, the chase column being three fixed 164px cells, 3 × 164 = 492. Appendix: 292 + 118 + 250 + 310 = 970 = 1012 − 2 border − 40 padding. Chase budget: 164 − 10 padding = 154px, about 27 characters; every Combination on this page is 27 characters or fewer, checked by script — the longest is T1 Minion Skills · T1 Life at 26 — so nothing in the chase column is cut. There is no overflow:hidden anywhere on this page.

Combinations read as tier, never as value (memlog 134). T1 Cold Res · T1 Mana, not +35% Cold Res · +180 Mana. The player operates on tiers and does not carry the value spreads in their head. The tier is the Accepted Tier the curator declared on the Tracked List (memlog 135) — never derived from the band, and never read from the Weights File's display-only tierLabel. A declared label may name a mixture, T1–T2, where AD-28 means the band cannot isolate one tier (memlog 137). The budget did not loosen when the values went: the modifier name was always the long part, and the change buys two to four characters on average and nothing at the tail.
