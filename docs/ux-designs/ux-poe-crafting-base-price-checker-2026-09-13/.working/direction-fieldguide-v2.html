Field guide, revision 2. Three things changed from v1. The 36px top-five band is gone: every ranked row is now 28px and every Base Type name is 14px, so rank 1 and rank 20 are the same height and the same size, and the pull to the top of the list is carried by weight alone — bold name and bold EV for ranks 1–5, a sepia rank numeral to mark that band, a mid-ink numeral for 6–10, quiet for the rest. The trust layer went silent: a row that is measured, fresh and drawn from a complete pool now carries no badge, no glyph and no word at all, so the six rows that are genuinely wrong are the only coloured things in a list of twenty and they are visible at an angled glance from across the desk. The width that the old always-on badges held has gone to the Chase Combinations, now a fixed 504px, three cells of 168px. Colour arrived in Terminal's restrained manner and stopped at three semantic inks — slate for split by model, ochre for prior only and unknown, rust for staleness and for what a patch broke — each always spoken as a word beside its glyph.

The resolution. The slot never holds a number-shaped placeholder; it holds a short phrase naming which question is open, and the four phrases are four different phrases — an open question, no figure yet, not valued, unknown. Why it works: FR-9's prohibition is aimed at marks that read as a quantity of zero, and a word is not a quantity, so the same slot can answer FR-4's demand for an unknown without ever looking like a price of nothing.
