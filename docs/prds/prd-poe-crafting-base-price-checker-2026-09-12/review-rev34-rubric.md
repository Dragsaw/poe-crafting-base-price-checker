# PRD Quality Review — PoE2 Crafting Base Price Checker (revision 4)

Reviewed against `.agents/skills/bmad-prd/assets/prd-validation-checklist.md`.
Target: `prd.md` rev 4 (822 lines, FR-1…FR-33), with `addendum.md` rev 4 as supporting context, and `ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md` rev 4 as the inherited layer.

Calibration: hobby/solo, one user who is also author and operator, chain-top (feeds epics and stories, and binds an external weights-producer project). Length and ceremony are judged against that. Per the review brief, arithmetic stated as a requirement in FR-1, FR-21, FR-23 and FR-29 is a recorded, justified decision (addendum, *Why the PRD Cites Architecture Decisions Instead of Restating Them*) and is **not** reported as implementation leakage; it is reported only where the same habit has spread to requirements the justification does not cover.

## Overall verdict

This is a strong PRD and the 31 absorbed edits landed cleanly: the valuation half — FR-1, FR-16, FR-21, FR-22, FR-27, FR-28, FR-29 — is now specified to a level where two independently built components genuinely cannot diverge, and the new AD-28 material (cells, cohorts, `cohortTotals`, interior-cell curation) is integrated rather than bolted on. What is at risk is the **presentation half**, which the previous pass already flagged and which this revision did not move: the view requirements still resolve to adjectives at exactly the points where the valuation requirements resolve to predicates, and two of them (FR-12's per-row age, FR-33's refusal-to-render) are unimplementable or unanswerable as written. The secondary risk is volume — the coverage gate, the `minChunkSearches` yardstick and the interior-cell rule are each argued in full in three to five places, and the reader who has to reconcile them is the same solo operator the ceremony was supposed to spare.

No blocking defect. Four high findings, all fixable inside a single editing pass.

## 1. Decision-readiness — **strong**

Decisions are stated as decisions and the trade-offs carry what was given up. FR-28's "there is deliberately no field for anonymous weight" names the rejected option and why; FR-26 names the identity distribution transform as a *documented limitation* and explicitly forbids an implementer filling the gap; §7.2 gives each deferral a revisit trigger rather than a shrug. §10's structure — resolved defects retained with their arguments, then four items raised and closed, then one item genuinely open — is the right shape and is honest about which is which.

OQ-12 is a real open question, not a rhetorical one: it is unanswered, owned outside the repository, and correctly classified as "blocking for correctness, not for building" with the consequence quantified (53 of 63 item classes silently mispriced). The three `[NOTE FOR PM]` callouts sit at real tensions (a spine bookkeeping discrepancy, a gap FR-18 does not close, a deferral that may need promotion).

### Findings
- **low** §0's "no citation went stale" is not earned (§0) — the paragraph asserts that no `(AD-n)` citation went stale and, two sentences later, that AD-9, AD-12 and AD-21 carry amended text the spine's own revision notes never announced. The PRD cites all three repeatedly (AD-9 in §3, FR-1, FR-9, FR-12, FR-17, FR-21, FR-24; AD-12 in FR-14, FR-25; AD-21 in FR-14, FR-25, NFR-5). The claim is about id stability, but it reads as a claim about content currency, which is precisely what the sentence beside it undermines. *Fix:* say what is true — "AD ids are stable, so no citation points at the wrong decision; three ADs carry content amendments the spine did not announce, and the citations to them were re-read against the current text."

## 2. Substance over theater — **strong**

No persona theater: §2 is five JTBD entries and a non-user list, each of which does work later (the "know when the answer has gone stale" job is the whole of §4.4; "keep a tool alive for a year" is NFR-7 and SM-5). No innovation section at all, correctly. The NFRs are product-specific with bounds where bounds exist — NFR-1's `onUnhandledRequest: "error"`, NFR-6's 100 ms, NFR-9's header-paced citizenship — and the one adjectival NFR (NFR-10, "accessibility floor") is scoped to a concrete prohibition rather than left as "accessible".

The Vision (§1) could not swap into another PRD: it names the specific failure (memorisation destroyed by league resets) and the specific counter-design (threshold as a dial, not a constant). §9's risks are all live and none is closed by a mitigation that does not exist — R-1 explicitly says "mitigation is honesty only … not correction", which is the opposite of theater.

### Findings
- **low** Ceremony exceeds the stated stakes, and the excess is rationale rather than requirement (throughout §4) — for a single-operator hobby tool, 33 FRs is defensible; ~400 lines of *why this rule exists* argument embedded inside the consequence lists is the cost. FR-17, FR-19 and FR-29 each spend more words defending the rule against a misreading than stating it. This is a judgment call the author has already made deliberately, so it is recorded as a note rather than a defect. *Fix:* if it is ever trimmed, move the "without this, two builders diverge" paragraphs to the addendum and leave a one-line pointer; the consequences themselves are already testable without them.

## 3. Strategic coherence — **strong**

The thesis is explicit and the document bets on it: *the ranking is not "most expensive base," and everything else in the system exists to make that distinction hold* (§1). Prioritisation follows — the threshold is a control (FR-6) because the thesis makes it one, Provenance and freshness are a whole feature (§4.4) because the thesis is undermined by a figure trusted past its warrant, and §7.2's deferrals are all things that do not serve the thesis yet (a second recipe adds no ordering information under an identity transform; mod-group conditioning is unestimable without measured weights).

Success Metrics validate the thesis rather than measuring activity — SM-1 through SM-4 are behavioural observations by the one user, honestly labelled as such, and all four counter-metrics are real counterweights with a named opposing SM. SM-C1 in particular is denominated in *searches* rather than entries specifically because entries would miss a band split, which is the same reasoning FR-14 carries. MVP scope kind is coherently problem-solving.

No findings.

## 4. Done-ness clarity — **thin**

This is the dimension that carries the review's weight, and it splits sharply.

The **valuation** requirements are as done as a PRD gets. FR-16's overlap predicate, FR-29's containment table plus the two edge-alignment equalities, FR-27's enumerated hard-error list, FR-4's `rankable`/`covered` predicates — each of these is testable with literal inputs, and each names the divergence it prevents. FR-22's two-step floor derivation with the bow worked example is directly implementable. FR-14's four-source table is exhaustive and closed ("a fifth source is an architecture amendment"). An engineer could build all of this without asking a question the PRD could have answered.

The **presentation** requirements are not at that standard, and the gap is structural rather than incidental. FR-8 specifies the expanded row's fields exactly — Combination, Price State, price, listing count, age, age-kind label — and nothing specifies the *collapsed* row, which is the surface most sessions actually read: not its fields, not EV's display precision, not tie-breaking when two Base Types have equal EV, not whether a Base Type whose EV is negative by its Craft Cost (FR-2's fourth consequence admits these exist) occupies a slot in FR-5's top 20. FR-10's three render treatments are "plain", "visibly distinct from both neighbours" and "visibly degraded", with NFR-10 supplying the only hard constraint. Most seriously, FR-4 sequences a measurement ahead of all view work, defines both halves of the fraction as predicates so that "two people who have never spoken" reproduce it — and then binds the result to "a **first-class surface** alongside the ranking, not a footer", which two such people will not reproduce. The rigour is spent on the gate and not on the thing the gate exists to decide.

Two presentation requirements are worse than vague — they cannot be satisfied as written (findings 1 and 2 below).

### Findings
- **high** FR-12's "an age in all four Price States" is unsatisfiable for a never-synced row (FR-12, §3 `lastAttemptedAt`) — §3 states `lastAttemptedAt` is "present in **all four** Price States"; FR-12 requires every row's age to come "from its `observedAt` where there is an observation and from its `lastAttemptedAt` otherwise". A `not-yet-synced` entry with reason `never-synced` has, by definition of `lastAttemptedAt` in the same glossary entry ("when `sync` last **issued a request**"), neither field. FR-17 handles this case separately by treating `not-yet-synced` as *infinitely old* for rotation purposes, but the view has no corresponding rule, so a builder either invents a null-age rendering or back-fills a timestamp that §3 forbids offline work from writing. *Fix:* narrow §3 to "present wherever a request has been issued — that is, in all four states except `not-yet-synced` with reason `never-synced`", and add an FR-12 consequence stating what such a row renders instead of an age ("never synced", not "0d").
- **high** FR-21 leaves the median convention open in the one requirement whose purpose is to stop divergence (FR-21, FR-23) — "the **median** of those listings' prices after normalisation to Divine" over a sample the same consequence caps at **10**. For an even sample, median-of-two-middle-values and lower-middle-value are both standard and give different numbers; FR-23 then says Divine prices are "rounded to 4 decimal places once, at normalisation", which does not say whether the median is taken over rounded values (and may itself land on a fifth decimal place when averaged) or before rounding. Every downstream figure inherits this, and it sits in the block of arithmetic the addendum justifies stating precisely *because* two components could diverge. *Fix:* one consequence — "median of an even-sized sample is the arithmetic mean of the two middle normalised values, computed before rounding; the result is rounded once to 4 dp."
- **high** FR-4's coverage gate binds a consequence that is not testable (FR-4 table, §7.3, §10 BQ-3) — the 50–80% band requires the Unrankable group become "a first-class surface alongside the ranking, not a footer". Every other term in FR-4 is a predicate; this one is a preference. Since the measurement is deliberately sequenced ahead of view work precisely so the layout is decided by data, the undefined half is the half that matters. *Fix:* state the two layouts concretely enough to be checked — e.g. footer = a collapsed section below the ranked list showing only a count; first-class = a persistent sibling section, visible without scrolling past the ranking, with the group's Base Types and reasons listed by default.
- **medium** FR-33's "refuses to render" has no stated user-facing outcome (FR-33) — `web` refuses to render a schema-invalid artifact rather than degrading. The FR goes to real trouble to prevent the *wrong* refusal (the cross-file qualification, so a `pinned` cap violation does not blank the site), which makes the absence of the right one conspicuous: for a genuinely invalid `dataset.json`, the player sees — what? A blank page, a message naming the artifact, a stale render? In a product whose premise is "a static page that always renders", this is the state the operator will actually meet after a bad sync commit. *Fix:* one consequence naming the failure surface — which artifact failed, which check, and that the rest of the page still renders where the failure is confined to one artifact.
- **medium** No requirement specifies the collapsed ranked row, EV presentation, or ranking ties (§4.1, FR-1, FR-2, FR-5) — FR-8 specifies the expanded row to the field; the collapsed row is defined only by what FR-2 adds to it. Unspecified and each a question a builder will ask: EV display precision and whether it is shown at all on the row, tie-breaking for equal EV (FR-17 bothers to specify a deterministic tie-break for the rotation; the ranking has none), and whether negative-EV Base Types are shown, ranked below zero-EV ones, or excluded from FR-5's 20. *Fix:* add a consequence to FR-1 fixing the tie-break (canonical `baseTypeId`, as FR-17 does for entries) and the treatment of negative EV; add a consequence to FR-2 listing the collapsed row's fields, the way FR-8 does for the expanded one.
- **medium** FR-12 does not say which clock a league-mismatched row reads (FR-12, FR-31, FR-9) — FR-31 makes `core` treat an observation from another league as `not-yet-synced`/`league-mismatch`. Such a row *does* carry an `observedAt` (last league's) and a `lastAttemptedAt`. FR-12's rule picks `observedAt` "where there is an observation", which would display last league's observation age as though it were current — the precise class of dishonesty §4.4 exists to prevent. *Fix:* an FR-12 consequence — a row whose observation is league-mismatched reads `lastAttemptedAt` and is labelled as such, because the observation it holds is not an observation of the active league.
- **medium** FR-7 and FR-11 disagree about banner dismissal (FR-7, FR-11) — FR-7: "the threshold **and view preferences** survive a page reload." FR-11: the uniform-prior banner is "persistent, **dismissible-per-session**". Whether a dismissal is a view preference that survives reload is exactly the question FR-7's undefined phrase invites and FR-11's phrase answers the other way. "View preferences" is also unenumerated — expanded rows? the FR-5 expand? *Fix:* enumerate what FR-7 persists, and state explicitly that the FR-11 dismissal is session-scoped and deliberately not among them.

## 5. Scope honesty — **strong**

§6 does real work: eleven non-goals, each with the reason it is out and several with the cost of being out named (the loot-filter entry concedes that the brief's "eventually I won't have to memorize anything" outcome **is not achievable**, which is the hardest kind of thing to write down). §7.2 distinguishes deferred-with-a-trigger from deferred-indefinitely. §7.3 is the right call: pulling the weights-file dependency out of FR-30 and stating it as a release gate is what stops it being discovered at integration, and the "`partial` is a recurring state, not a transitional one" paragraph pre-empts the optimistic reading.

Open-items density is appropriate and falling: ten indexed assumptions, three `[NOTE FOR PM]` callouts, one genuinely open question, on a document that is green-lit to build. All ten assumptions are inferences the user did not directly confirm, and none is load-bearing enough to block.

### Findings
- **medium** FR-30 silently improves on AD-11 without flagging it (FR-30, §7.3 vs AD-11) — AD-11 states "Until a conforming file exists, **every base** is unrankable by AD-18." FR-30 states every **crafted** Base Type is Unrankable and Raw Bases are the exception, "so what survives a missing file is a white-base price list, not the product." FR-30 is correct — FR-3 gives a Raw Base no probability term — but this is a substantive divergence from an inherited decision in a document that elsewhere routes every such divergence into §10 and back at the spine. Leaving it unflagged sets the precedent the §10 discipline exists to prevent. *Fix:* either a §10 entry raising AD-11's over-broad sentence against the spine, or a parenthetical in FR-30 noting the refinement and that AD-11's wording predates AD-17's raw-base branch.

## 6. Downstream usability — **adequate**

The Glossary is present, thorough, and used verbatim — a spot-check of Base Type, Tracked Entry, Combination, Eligible Pool, Cohort, Provenance and Chunk found no synonym drift across §4. FR ids are contiguous FR-1…FR-33 with no gaps or duplicates; UJ-1…UJ-6, SM-1…SM-6 and SM-C1…SM-C4 likewise; every cross-reference checked (FR-n, AD-n, §10 items, NFR-n) resolves. Sections pull out alone: FR-29 read cold is implementable without reading FR-27 first.

What holds this at adequate rather than strong is repetition, which is a downstream cost rather than a reading one. Three rules are now argued in full in three or more places, and a story-generation pass that source-extracts from any one of them will produce duplicate or conflicting stories.

### Findings
- **medium** Three rules are stated in full in three to five places each (§0, §3, FR-4, FR-14, FR-17, FR-19, FR-21, FR-22, FR-27, FR-31, §7.3, §10) — (a) the **pool-coverage gate** — predicates, bands and the recurrence argument — appears in §0's preamble, FR-4 (with the full derivation), §7.3 ("its first gate is a measurement") and §10 BQ-3, which then says "FR-4 states it normatively and is the copy to build to", conceding the duplication; (b) **`minChunkSearches` is a yardstick, not a Chunk bound** is argued in §3's *Chunk* entry, FR-14, FR-17, FR-19 (two long consequences) and FR-31; (c) the **interior-cell curation rule** and its BQ-1-through-a-heterogeneous-band justification appear in §3's *Accepted Tier*, FR-21's last consequence and FR-22's fourth-from-last, near-verbatim. In each case one statement plus pointers would carry the same information and would not require three edits to stay consistent. *Fix:* pick the normative home (FR-4, FR-19, FR-22 respectively), reduce the others to a one-line citation, and let §10 and §7.3 carry only what is unique to them — the argument and the release framing.
- **low** `cohortTotals`' trigger condition is narrower in the PRD than in the spine (FR-27, §3) — FR-27's hard-error list requires "a missing `cohortTotals` row for a family carrying a `modelled-split` entry". AD-28 requires it "per decomposed family" and explicitly allows a producer that *measures* cell masses directly to carry `measured` rather than `modelled-split`. Under FR-27's wording, a decomposed family whose cells are `measured` needs no `cohortTotals` row and so escapes what §3 and FR-27 both call "the **only** mechanical check on mass conservation" — for the denominator of every affected Base Type. *Fix:* trigger on decomposition, not on provenance: "a missing `cohortTotals` row for any family emitting more than one cell per cohort".

## 7. Shape fit — **strong**

The shape is right. Single operator, single role, no auth, no handoff — and §2.3 says so and then writes the UJs in the lighter form rather than manufacturing named personas for an audience of one. Six UJs is the correct density: each drives at least one feature (UJ-5 is the whole of §4.5's read-only curation surfacing; UJ-6 is §4.9) and none is floating. SMs are appropriately behavioural-and-uninstrumented with the reason stated.

Chain-top is handled well: the document knows it feeds both a story workflow and an external producer project, and FR-28's "this obligation binds an **external project**, and this document plus `WEIGHTS-FILE-SCHEMA.md` are its entire contract" is exactly the sentence that makes that work.

### Findings
- **low** UJ-2 presumes a control FR-6 does not specify (UJ-2, FR-6) — "**drags** the threshold from a quarter of a Divine to one Divine" implies a slider and continuous re-ranking during the drag; FR-6 says only that the player "sets a Payout Threshold in Divine" and that the list re-ranks synchronously. Given NFR-6's 100 ms budget the drag reading is affordable, but it is an inference. *Fix:* either name the control in FR-6 or soften UJ-2 to "changes".
- **low** Three expand affordances, no stated interaction (FR-4, FR-5, FR-8) — the Unrankable group expands, the ranked list expands past 20, and each row expands to its Combinations. Whether expanding the list resets row expansions, and whether the Unrankable group's members are themselves expandable to their tracked entries, is unstated. Low, because any reasonable answer works. *Fix:* one line in FR-8 stating whether Unrankable Base Types are expandable.

FR-2 carries two inline tags folded into one index entry, which is fine. One ordering nit: the FR-21 entry sits between FR-4 and FR-5, out of FR order and out of the §-order the list otherwise follows — it reads as a late insertion. *Fix:* move it after the FR-18 entry.
- **ID continuity is clean.** FR-1…FR-33 contiguous and unique; NFR-1…NFR-10; UJ-1…UJ-6; SM-1…SM-6, SM-C1…SM-C4; R-1…R-7; §10 items 1–12 (BQ-1…BQ-3, OQ-4…OQ-12) with no gap. All AD citations fall within AD-1…AD-28, which the spine defines.
